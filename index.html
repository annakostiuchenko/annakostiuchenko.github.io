A/B testing

Campaign Performance · A/B Test 2025

A marketing experiment report comparing Control and Test variants of two campaigns across 323.5K audience entries. It tracks CTR month by month, compares conversion rates, and checks whether the difference is real with a two-proportion Z-test.

+15.3%conversion uplift, Test vs Control
9.10%best variant: Campaign B Test
p=0.0064below the 0.05 threshold

Key insights

  • Test variants beat Control every month on CTR, rising from 3.75% to 4.8% by December.
  • Campaign B's Test group converts best (9.10% vs 8.35% for Campaign A).
  • Recommendation: prioritise the Test variants, especially B, and confirm with a live randomized experiment before full rollout.

Campaign funnel · demo data

Promo Campaign Funnel · Birthday & Holidays

A report inspired by my real work at PUMB. To keep security in mind, it uses fake data. It follows two promo campaigns from audience to offer. A matrix breaks every campaign down by banner and shows each funnel step, from moving to the Credits section to seeing a banner, clicking it and using the offer. Slicers filter by month, campaign, banner, test group and product.

18,600clients across both campaigns
32%moved on to the Credits section
411clients used the offer

What it shows

  • The funnel matrix uses in-cell data bars, so the strongest banners stand out at a glance.
  • Conversion is compared between the control and main groups for each banner.
  • A Z-test per product checks whether the main group really converts better. None of the differences were significant (total p = 0.14).
